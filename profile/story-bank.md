# Story bank

STAR+R interview stories, accumulated over time. Block F of every report at 4.0 and up adds new ones here. Instead of memorizing a hundred answers, keep five to ten deep stories you can bend to almost any behavioral question.

## How it works

1. The evaluate skill appends stories as it writes interview plans.
2. Before an interview, read this file; the stories are already organized by theme.
3. The big three questions are combinations of what is here: "tell me about yourself" is two or three stories as a narrative, "your most impactful project" is the highest-impact story, "a conflict you resolved" is any story with a reflection.

### [Scale] Example: took a team from X to Y without adding headcount
**Source:** written by hand
**S (Situation):** The team was producing X a year with a process that broke at every handoff.
**T (Task):** Double output within a year on the same budget.
**A (Action):** Mapped the handoffs, removed two, moved the review step earlier, trained the leads on the new sequence.
**R (Result):** Y a year by month ten; the review step caught problems a week earlier.
**Reflection:** The process was the product. The tooling came second.
**Best for questions about:** scaling, process, leading change, doing more with less

## Red-flag answers (Block F)
