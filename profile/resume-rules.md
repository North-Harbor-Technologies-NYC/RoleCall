# Resume rules

These apply to every resume, cover letter, and application answer the system produces. They are standing rules, not suggestions. Edit the section order and the file name to match your master; keep the writing rules.

## Formatting

- Red text for anything new or changed. Black text is the preserved master. A reviewer must be able to see at a glance what was tailored.
- Company line format: `Company Name - Location | Dates`. Plain hyphen between company and location, pipe before dates, no tab stops.
- Job title line sits directly under the company line. No blank line, no spacing between them.
- Never use em dashes. Use a comma, a period, or restructure.
- Two pages, US Letter, Arial. Name centered and bold, one-line positioning header beneath it, contact line beneath that.
- Section order: PROFILE, TOOLS, EXPERIENCE, ADDITIONAL EXPERIENCE. Do not add sections without asking.
- File name: `<First>_<Last>_Resume_[Company]`, with `_[Role]` or `_v2` suffixes only when needed.

## Writing

- Lead readers to the conclusion. Do not restate the job description's phrasing. If the JD says "cross-functional leadership," show the thirty concurrent projects and the named partners, and let the reader name it.
- ATS keyword density should feel natural. A keyword belongs only where a human writing this bullet would have used it.
- Specifics over abstractions: numbers, names of clients, named projects, named tools.
- Short sentences. Action verbs. Active voice.
- Vary bullet openings. Do not start every bullet with "Led."
- Banned words and phrases: passionate about, results-oriented, proven track record, leveraged, spearheaded, facilitated, synergies, robust, seamless, cutting-edge, innovative, fast-paced, demonstrated ability to, best practices, genuinely, honestly, straightforward.

## What is allowed to change per application

- The positioning header line.
- The PROFILE paragraph.
- Bullet order within a role.
- Up to three bullets per role rewritten for emphasis, provided every fact traces to the master.
- The TOOLS line, reordered or trimmed, never padded with tools you have not used.

## What is not allowed to change

- Titles, companies, locations, dates.
- Any metric.
- Credits, client names, project names.
- Adding a role, a degree, a certification, or an award that is not in the master.

## Standing decisions

Record here the calls you make about your own resume so the system never relitigates them. Examples of the kind of thing that belongs: whether education is listed; whether memberships are listed; how an overlap between two roles is explained (a spoken answer in `story-bank.md`, never a resume edit); whether relationships or referrals ever appear on paper (recommended: never; use them directly).
